Sketch Heat pricing

Sketch Heat is free to download and to use with its free features. Sketch Heat Pro is a subscription, monthly or yearly, with a free trial for new subscribers.

Free and Sketch Heat Pro

Free

  • Run and explore every example, in every analysis.
  • Draw your own outline and holes in a plate of aluminum 6061-T6 or copper, with a hot edge and a cooled edge, in steady heat.
  • Coarse and Medium meshes.
  • Projects, files, units, and the help.
  • View the sample paper drawing.

Sketch Heat Pro adds

  • Every condition: heat flux, heat sources, radiation, fluid streams, contact and symmetry, and probes.
  • Every material, and your own materials.
  • Heat over time, melting and freezing, and diffusion.
  • Convection worked out from the situation: still or moving air, water or oil.
  • Fine meshes for more detail.
  • Edit the examples.
  • Import your own paper drawings from a photo.
  • Reports with the model settings and probe readings.

In the iPhone and iPad app

  • Yearly: US$49.99 a year, with a 14-day free trial.
  • Monthly: US$4.99 a month, with a 7-day free trial.

These are the prices in the United States. In other countries the App Store shows the price in your currency, and it may include taxes. Apple bills the subscription to your Apple Account, and free trials are for eligible new subscribers. Refunds for purchases in the app are handled by Apple, as the Refund Policy says.

Canceling

You can cancel at any time. Sketch Heat Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, and your sketches still open and run after it ends. Cancel in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the free trial or the current period ends.
